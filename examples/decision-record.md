# Example decision record

## Decision question

Should a support team use an AI assistant to draft replies to common customer questions?

## Context and constraints

The team wants faster first drafts. Customer messages may contain personal data. A human must approve every reply before it is sent.

## What the perspectives agree on

- Start with a limited set of low-risk questions.
- Use approved source material rather than open-ended model knowledge.
- Keep the draft separate from the send action.
- Measure correction time as well as drafting time.

## Dissent and unresolved assumptions

One perspective recommends a broad pilot. Two perspectives recommend a narrow pilot because the quality of the source material is not yet known. The disagreement is unresolved.

## Evidence and source gaps

The team still needs an anonymised sample of real questions and an agreed list of approved answers. No productivity result is claimed before that test.

## Recommendation and safer alternative

Run a two-week test on one category of low-risk questions. The assistant may draft, but cannot send. A reviewer records whether the draft was accepted, corrected, or rejected.

## Human approval needed from

The support lead approves the category, source set, and test result before expansion.

## Next test or action

Prepare 50 anonymised questions and define the acceptance criteria before selecting a model.
